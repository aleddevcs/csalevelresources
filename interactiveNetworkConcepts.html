MAC Address Insight

Before your packet leaves the building, your router uses its MAC Address to move data locally. Spoofing involves changing this ID to trick a network switch into thinking you are a trusted device (like a printer or admin laptop).

How TTL Works

Every router a packet hits reduces the Time-to-Live (TTL) by 1. When it hits 0, the router kills the packet and tells you who it is. This is how we map the breadcrumbs!

IP Masking (VPN)

Masking wraps your original IP in another layer. To the world, the traceroute looks like it starts from the VPN server, not your home.
