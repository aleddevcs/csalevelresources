Question 3 — a dictionary. What does this print?
prices = {"tea": 2, "coffee": 3}
print(prices["coffee"])
Question 4 — looping through it. What does this print?
total = 0
for item, price in prices.items():
    total = total + price
print(total)
Hold that thought: a dictionary finds a value by its label — prices["coffee"]. Today's new structure finds values by their position.
