Section 4: Prototype Guide

Part 1

Checklist & Document Structure

Prototype – 10 marks

The candidate has:

  • Justified the choice of areas to be included in the prototype system and explained the reasons for omitting the remaining areas
  • Created a comprehensive range of screens and outputs for the chosen areas of the solution
  • Created a functioning system that carries out all chosen processes using realistic data and fulfils all requirements for data output and storage
  • Evaluated the functioning of the prototype and justified the good features of the prototype solution
  • Described the shortcomings and made specific suggestions for improvement
1. Introduction

What will you be doing in this stage of the software development.

2. Justification
  • Inclusions: What areas are you going to include in your prototype? (Bullet points with justifications)
  • Omissions: What areas have you left out? (Bullet points with justifications)
3. Evidence

Provide screenshots showing all chosen areas working:

  • Range of screens and outputs
  • Functioning system processes
  • Use of realistic data for output and storage
4. Evaluation
  • Evaluate the prototype solution.
  • Make specific suggestions for improvement.

Part 3

Marking Grid (AO3.1b)

GCE AS and A Level Computer Science 42 - WJEC CBAC Ltd.

Band Marks Descriptor
3 8-10 marks

The candidate has:

  • Justified the choice of areas to be included in the prototype system and explained reasons for omitting the remaining areas
  • Created a comprehensive range of screens and outputs for the chosen areas of the solution
  • Created a functioning system that carries out all chosen processes using realistic data and fulfils all requirements for data output and storage
  • Evaluated the functioning of the prototype and justified the good features of the solution
  • Described the shortcomings and made specific suggestions for improvement.
2 4-7 marks

The candidate has:

  • Described the areas to be included in the prototype system
  • Created mock ups of screens and outputs related to all areas described
  • Created a system that carries out most of the chosen system processes using realistic data to create required outputs
  • Considered the functioning of the prototype system and described the good features and identified the shortcomings and made suggestions for some improvements.
1 1-3 marks

The candidate has:

  • Identified some areas of the proposed solution that are included in the prototype solution
  • Created mock ups of the main screens and essential outputs
  • Simulated some systems processes but does not use real data.
0 0 marks Response not credit worthy or not attempted.
