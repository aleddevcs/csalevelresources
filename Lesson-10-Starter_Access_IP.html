2Quick retrieval — Lesson 9

What are the three steps of the priming read?
1. Ask once, before the loop. 2. Test the answer in the while condition. 3. Ask again, inside the loop.
Why does while name == "done": not work as a sentinel loop?
The condition means "keep going while this is true". So == keeps looping only while the answer IS the stop word. You want !=.
for or while: collecting entries until the user types a stop word?
while — you don't know in advance how many entries there will be, so it's an indeterminate loop.
What makes a loop infinite?
Its condition never becomes false — nothing inside the loop changes the thing the condition depends on.
