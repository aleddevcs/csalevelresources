A system isn't one process — break it into parts

Last lesson you IPO-mapped a single process: chasing overdue books. A real current system is usually made up of several distinct parts, each with its own inputs, processes and outputs. Below are two more parts of Ysgol Cwm Coleg's library system — cataloguing new stock and registering a new member. Same fictional library scenario as the rest of the Investigation resources. When you do this for your own system, split it into its own parts too — e.g. placing an order, processing a return, generating a report — and IPO-map each part on its own.

Watch for this

A few highlighted phrases describe what the library wishes it could do — not what it actually does right now. Those don't belong in a current-system IPO list at all, however tempting they look. Your Investigation records what is happening; ideas for the new system belong in Design.
